# Geography sources

## US states (choropleth polygons)

- **Product**: U.S. Census Bureau Cartographic Boundary Files
- **Layer**: States, 1:5,000,000 (`cb_2025_us_state_5m`)
- **Vintage**: 2025
- **CRS**: WGS84 (EPSG:4326), RFC7946 GeoJSON
- **Download**: https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/GENZ2025/shp/cb_2025_us_state_5m.zip
- **Program page**: https://www.census.gov/geographies/mapping-files/time-series/geo/cartographic-boundary.html
- **License**: U.S. government work, public domain
- **Use**: These files are the Census product designed for small-scale thematic mapping (true choropleth). They are simplified from TIGER/Line; they are not survey-accurate cadastral boundaries.

City points use representative lat/lon (not place polygons). Neighborhood polygons are not loaded; public medication rates almost never exist at that level.
