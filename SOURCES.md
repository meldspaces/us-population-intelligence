# Data Sources

**Policy**: Only aggregate public statistics. No individual or personally identifiable information.

## Primary Sources with Links

1. **U.S. Census Bureau – City Populations (Vintage 2025)**  
   Annual Estimates of the Resident Population for Incorporated Places.  
   https://www.census.gov/data/tables/time-series/demo/popest/2020s-total-cities-and-towns.html  
   https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest.html  
   Ranked lists also referenced via https://www.everycityintheusa.com/cities/over-50000/

2. **CDC / NCHS – National Health Interview Survey 2023**  
   Data Brief No. 528: Characteristics of Adults Age 18 and Older Who Took Prescription Medication for Depression: United States, 2023.  
   https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db528.htm  
   Tables: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK617971/

3. **Perlis et al. 2026 – 50-State Survey**  
   “Antidepressant use among American adults in a 50-state survey.” BMJ Mental Health 2026;29:e302287.  
   https://mentalhealth.bmj.com/content/29/1/e302287  
   PMC: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12829365/

4. **U.S. Census Bureau / NCHS – Household Pulse Survey**  
   Mental health care indicators (prescription medication for mental health).  
   https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/covid19/pulse/mental-health-care.htm

5. **CDC / NCHS – NHANES reference (2015–2018)**  
   Data Brief 377: Antidepressant Use Among Adults: United States, 2015–2018.  
   https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db377.htm

6. **Express Scripts – America’s State of Mind Report (2019 data, published 2020)**  
   Historical commercial pharmacy claims for antidepressants by state.

All medication figures are **proxies** (antidepressant use or medication for depression). Exact city-level SSRI prevalence is not publicly available in standardized form.
